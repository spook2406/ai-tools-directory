About ToolNest

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ToolNest is an independent, English-language directory of free and freemium AI tools and developer utilities. We curate links so founders, students, and makers can quickly find official sites — we do not host the tools themselves.

Editorial notes

  • Listings focus on tools with a usable free tier or fully free web apps.
  • Brand names belong to their owners; ToolNest is not affiliated with listed products.
  • We may earn revenue via display ads (e.g. Google AdSense) once approved. Affiliate links may be added later and will be disclosed.
